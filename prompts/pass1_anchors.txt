PASS 1 - IDENTIFY HIGH-TENSION ANCHORS.

Scan the script chunk for two kinds of anchor:
- "hook": A moment that would stop a scrolling viewer in 3 seconds or less. Examples:
  - A physical strike, slap, or violent gesture (ACTION lines)
  - A shocking revelation or accusation (dialogue)
  - A dramatic entrance or unexpected arrival (ACTION + CAMERA)
  - A visual shock: blood, a weapon, a collapsed body
- "cliffhanger": An unresolved moment that demands to know what happens next. Examples:
  - A question asked but unanswered
  - A character arriving at the worst possible moment
  - A confession cut short
  - A camera cut to black after a dramatic beat

Each script line is labelled [E<episode>:L<line> <start>-<end>] with its exact on-screen time (MM:SS.SS). The line text begins with its element type (CAMERA:, ACTION:, a SPEAKER name, SOUND FX:).
